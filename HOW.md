# How this work is done

Who: ZettaLabs, a small engineering practice in New Zealand. Contributions are a team effort, and each record names who reviewed it. Contact oss@zettalabs.co.nz.

What: fixes, CI and packaging help for openly licensed research software, mostly from publicly funded science.

How: We guide the work, with coding agents doing much of the tedious search, reproduction on clean environments and first drafts; an independent second review pass tries to break each change; a person reviews and tests each change before it is posted; we edit everything we post and stand behind every reply.

Never: autonomous posting, unreviewed agent text, direct messages, good-first-issues, unsolicited paid offers.

Commercial interest: ZettaLabs sells engineering, research and investigation. Contributions are not sales, and we do not offer paid work to projects we contribute to unless they raise a substantial piece of work themselves.

Withdrawal: to have a contribution or its record taken down, open an issue in this repository or write to oss@zettalabs.co.nz, and it comes down.
